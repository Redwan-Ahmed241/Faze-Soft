-

What is the optional monthly Growth Management?

+
-

Our monthly management programs (+$299/mo for Growth System and +$599/mo for Market Leader) are 100% optional. They provide continuous organic rank optimization, keyword tracking, new content publications, technical performance monitoring, security maintenance, and monthly growth consulting.

What is AEO foundation?

+
-

AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimization. We structure your FAQ and website schema markup using Schema.org JSON-LD microdata so modern AI answer engines (like Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and ChatGPT) can parse, cite, and recommend your services as trusted answers.

What is the delivery timeline?

+
-

Standard turnaround is 10 to 14 business days for Launch System, 14 to 21 business days for Growth System, and 3 to 5 weeks for Market Leader System, depending on scope and client asset readiness.
