Zero Tracking Guarantee

FazeSoft does not use intrusive cross-site tracking cookies, retargeting pixels, or third-party advertising brokers. Your browsing stays private.

Read Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 4, 2026 • Version: 2.0

This Cookie Policy explains how FazeSoft ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies, local browser storage, HTTP caching headers, and related web technologies on fazesoft.com to provide a responsive, reliable, and secure user experience.

1. What Are Cookies & Web Storage Technologies?

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device by a web server when you visit a website. Cookies allow sites to recognize your browser, maintain preferences across sessions, and enable functional features.

In addition to standard HTTP cookies, modern web applications may utilize browser-native storage mechanisms such as localStorage, sessionStorage, in-memory script state, and HTTP Cache-Control headers to optimize loading speeds and transition smoothness without repeatedly requesting redundant assets from the server.

2. How FazeSoft Uses Storage Technologies

We employ web storage and caching exclusively for operational, performance, and functional purposes:

  • Smooth Page Transitions: Managing Single-Page Application (SPA) navigation transitions via Swup.js so pages load instantly without jarring white flashes.
  • Interactive Form States: Retaining package selections when navigating from our pricing comparison cards to the Client Portal onboarding form.
  • Anti-Abuse Verification: Evaluating honeypot validation tokens during form submissions to prevent automated bot spam.
  • Network Efficiency: Directing your browser to cache static assets (stylesheets, JavaScript bundles, fonts, WebP graphics) locally so subsequent page views render immediately.

3. Swup.js & Session Behavior

Our website incorporates Swup, a lightweight page transition engine. Here is how it operates under the hood:

  • HTML5 History API: When you click internal links, Swup intercepts the navigation, fetches the target page container via background AJAX, and updates the browser address bar using window.history.pushState(). This keeps browser back/forward navigation fully operational.
  • In-Memory Volatile Cache: Swup temporarily caches page fragments in your browser's active RAM memory during your active browsing session. This memory is completely cleared as soon as you close your browser tab or window. It does not track your identity across different websites.
  • Package Booking Pre-Selection: In the Client Portal, clicking a "Book This Package" button triggers an event listener in main.js that pre-selects the corresponding tier in the dropdown menu (e.g., Launch $999, Growth $1,499, or Market Leader $3,499). This state is held in transient DOM memory and is not stored permanently.

4. Static Caching & Edge Delivery

Our edge infrastructure (Vercel) configures strict HTTP Cache-Control headers to optimize website performance while preserving your privacy:

  • /img/ and /fonts/: Configured with public, max-age=2592000, stale-while-revalidate=86400 (cached locally for 30 days to eliminate duplicate asset downloads).
  • /css/ and /js/: Configured with public, max-age=86400, stale-while-revalidate=604800 (cached for 24 hours to ensure snappy animations while guaranteeing you receive code updates promptly).
  • Security Headers: Every response includes X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff and Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin to protect against MIME confusion and cross-site referrer leakage.

5. Third-Party Embeds & External APIs

Certain interactive components on our website interface with third-party technical providers:

  • Google Fonts: We preconnect to fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com to render the Outfit typeface. Google Fonts does not set cookies, but your browser transmits its IP address to Google's CDN to download font binaries.
  • Google Maps Iframe (Contact Page): We embed an interactive map on contact.html to showcase our location. When loaded, Google may set session preferences or security cookies in your browser in accordance with Google's Cookie Policy.
  • FormSubmit API: Form submissions are posted via HTTPS to FormSubmit for transit to our email inbox. FormSubmit does not set persistent tracking cookies on your device.
  • Social Links: Links to our LinkedIn and Facebook pages include target="_blank", rel="noopener noreferrer", and data-no-swup to ensure clean sandboxing and prevent transition conflicts.

6. What We Do NOT Track

As a high-end engineering agency, we respect developer and business privacy:

  • We do NOT run Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, or cross-site behavioral retargeting beacons.
  • We do NOT sell, broker, or syndicate visitor browsing habits to data brokers.
  • We do NOT engage in intrusive device fingerprinting.

8. Managing & Disabling Storage Technologies

You have full control over cookie and storage behaviors in your browser settings. You can review, block, or delete cookies at any time:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies.

If you disable cookies or clear your cache, our website will remain completely accessible, though static assets will re-download upon each visit.
