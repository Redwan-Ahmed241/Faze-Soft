Ask our team

Effective Date: November 4, 2026 • Version: 2.0

Welcome to FazeSoft ("we", "our", or "us"). We specialize in custom software engineering, AI application development, enterprise web systems, and dedicated digital solutions for businesses globally. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, store, and safeguard your personal and business information across our website (fazesoft.com) and client intake systems.

1. Introduction & Scope

This policy applies to all visitors, clients, prospective clients, and subscribers interacting with FazeSoft digital touchpoints. We treat all data provided to us with the highest standards of professional discretion, adhering to transparent data intake protocols and international privacy best practices.

By accessing our website, submitting an inquiry via our contact form, requesting an onboarding kickoff in our Client Portal, or subscribing to our engineering newsletter, you acknowledge the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary information required to understand your engineering requirements, deliver requested proposals, and communicate during project lifecycles:

A. Contact & Discovery Inquiries

Collected via our Contact Form (data-fs-form="contact"): your full name, work email address, and message detailing your software or AI project specifications.

B. Client Portal Onboarding & Booking Payloads

Collected via our Client Portal Booking Form (data-fs-form="booking"): selected system tier (Launch System $999, Growth System $1,499, Market Leader System $3,499, or custom enterprise architecture), client contact name, work email, company name, existing or target domain URL, and specific architectural or design requirements.

C. Newsletter Subscriptions

Collected via our Footer Newsletter Module (data-fs-form="newsletter"): your business email address for software architecture trends, framework releases, and agency insights.

D. Technical & Transmission Metadata

Whenever an inquiry is submitted, our client-side script packages operational transmission metadata including the originating webpage URL (Submitted from), submission timestamp, and honeypot validation tokens to defeat automated spam bots.

Note: We do not process or store sensitive payment card information on this website. Commercial invoicing and project milestone transactions are executed through direct institutional bank wire, secure payment gateways, or verified escrow agreements.

3. How Inquiries Are Processed (FormSubmit & Mailto Fallback)

To maintain zero downtime and ensure client inquiries are never misplaced or dropped, our platform integrates a modern, secure intake pipeline:

  • Asynchronous JSON Relay: Inquiries are transmitted via encrypted HTTPS AJAX POST requests to FormSubmit (https://formsubmit.co/ajax/fazesoft.2024@gmail.com). FormSubmit acts strictly as an operational transmission relay, packaging the submitted form fields into an encrypted structured email notification delivered immediately to our authorized engineering mailbox.
  • Honeypot Spam Elimination: Every form includes an invisible anti-spam honeypot field (_honey). Submissions triggered by automated scraping bots are silently filtered out prior to transmission.
  • Fail-Safe Mailto Fallback: If the FormSubmit endpoint is temporarily unreachable or blocked by client-side firewall restrictions, our script automatically constructs a pre-formatted mailto: fallback link opening your default mail application directly addressed to fazesoft.2024@gmail.com, ensuring direct point-to-point delivery without third-party transit.
"We respect your inbox and time. Our direct developer response is guaranteed within 12 hours for all verified system kickoff and onboarding submissions."

4. Purposes of Data Processing

We process submitted client data solely for lawful, legitimate business objectives:

  • Reviewing technical project briefs and preparing scoped proposals, system architectures, and price quotes.
  • Scheduling kickoff sessions, discovery interviews, and development sprints.
  • Sending onboarding packages, sprint milestone demonstrations, and staging server access.
  • Distributing requested newsletters and agency announcements (with one-click unsubscribe).
  • Fulfilling legal, accounting, and contractual obligations under mutual development agreements.

5. Data Retention & Storage

FazeSoft adheres to data minimization and responsible retention principles:

  • General Inquiries: Inquiries that do not materialize into an active engagement are securely archived for up to 12 months for commercial reference, after which they are permanently deleted upon audit.
  • Active Client Engagements: Commercial records, contracts, statements of work, and invoicing history are retained for the statutory period required by corporate accounting regulations (typically 5 to 7 years).
  • Development Artifacts: Staging environments, temporary test databases, and client credentials provided during active development are revoked, wiped, or handed over completely to the client upon project sign-off and warranty expiration.
  • Newsletter Subscribers: Stored until you unsubscribe or request removal via email.

6. Client Code & Project Confidentiality

As a software engineering agency handling proprietary technology, algorithms, and business systems, confidentiality is our highest operational imperative:

  • Non-Disclosure (NDA): We routinely execute comprehensive bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreements prior to receiving proprietary source code, internal APIs, or confidential commercial documentation.
  • Repository Isolation: Code repositories (GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket) are partitioned with role-based access control (RBAC). Only engineers assigned to your specific milestone retain repository access.
  • Source Code Ownership: Upon full settlement of agreed project milestone invoices, all custom intellectual property and source code produced for your project belong 100% to you. We never repurpose client proprietary business logic.

7. Third-Party Services

We employ vetted, industry-leading infrastructure providers to deliver our web platform and services:

  • Hosting & Edge Delivery: Vercel Inc. (global edge network, SSL encryption, security headers).
  • Form Ingestion Relay: FormSubmit (transient submission packaging and email relay).
  • Typography: Google Fonts (served via Google's edge cache).
  • Interactive Maps: Google Maps iframe embed on our Contact page (governed by Google's Privacy Policy).

We do not authorize any third party to access, sell, or monetize your information for advertising or cross-context tracking.

8. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on your geographic location (including rights under the European Union GDPR, UK GDPR, and other international data privacy frameworks), you possess the following rights:

  • Access: The right to request copies of personal data we maintain concerning you.
  • Rectification: The right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete records.
  • Erasure: The right to request deletion of your personal records ("Right to be Forgotten"), subject to ongoing contractual or statutory recordkeeping mandates.
  • Data Portability: The right to receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Objection & Restriction: The right to object to or restrict processing of your contact details for direct marketing communications.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at fazesoft.2024@gmail.com. We respond to all formal verification requests within 30 calendar days.
